Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to draw pictures with her crayons. One day, she found a picture of a cat. The cat had a hurt leg. Lily wanted to help the cat heal.
Lily asked her mom for a soft thing. Her mom said, "We will put a soft thing on it to make it feel better." They went to the cat's home. Lily gave the cat some soft food and a soft bed to sleep on.
The cat's leg was better. It was not a cat anymore. Lily and her mom took the cat home. They put a soft bed on the cat's leg. The cat was happy and healthy. Lily felt good because she helped the cat heal.
